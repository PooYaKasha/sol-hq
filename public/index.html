    Decisions · the Go list

    Case studies

    Every finished campaign is a case study, and every year-end promo campaign is checked against the 5% guarantee.

    5% guarantee check · year-end promo
    The year-end promo measures two things against benchmarks: donation conversion and donor acquisition rate. If neither improves, MatchNice donates its fee back to the nonprofit. A campaign is covered only if it ran on a MatchNice integration and the nonprofit agreed to benchmark measurement. A human confirms every verdict; Penny drafts the fee donation and a founder sends it.
    All campaign results
    Case studies use the same fields as every campaign (result, match, donors, hours to deliver), so one finished campaign becomes a case study without new work. Dashes are fields not yet recorded.

    Admin · give an investor access

    Send a sign-in link. No password.

    1. You add them. Only emails on this list can ask for a link.
    2. They ask for a link. They open the investor page, type their email and tap "Email me a link".
